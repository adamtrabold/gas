THE DIAL ON A TALL PHONE — throwaway sketch, jam pass 2 (visual)

Real sun geometry at 35°N. No outlines anywhere: every black is a stack of tapered strokes laid in the direction the light travels (by night: paper strokes laid away from the lamp, and star tails laid round the pole). Station faces south, 10 m sign pole, viewer on the road 14.3 m south of the pole, eye height 1.7 m. Drawn in 2D with a hand-rolled projection, not three.js: this tests the picture, not the build. Lettering is a stand-in.
