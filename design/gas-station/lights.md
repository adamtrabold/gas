# Lights — every real light in the place

Your words: "Lighting should work as in the real world", and "The lighting thing means we need to establish proper scene lights."

This is the list of them, made the way the element list was. **Against any line say *wrong*, *missing* or *strike*.**

**The rule.** These are all the lights there are. Each lights what it really would. A thing, or you, throws a shadow from it if it's the kind of light that makes one. Where none of them reaches, it is dark. Nothing follows where you look.

## Not real light, kept on purpose

For the design director to confirm; they are in the concept and they break your rule knowingly.

- **A patch of light on the ground with no lamp over it.** The alien material. In a place where every light has a source, the one without is the evidence — [concept]
- **A light that crosses the forecourt after four minutes of standing still,** from nothing overhead — [concept]
- **"One left on":** a light indoors that you didn't switch on. The light itself is real; that it's on is not yours — [concept]
- **Lights can't be seen from more than about 100 m off.** A real sign would be seen for miles. It hides where the world joins up — [concept]
- **At dusk the name comes on a letter a second.** Real signs come on at dusk; the pace is ours — [concept]
- **The wall clock moves the sun** — [concept]

## One question

**Is there a moon?** With one, on some nights the desert is dim but you can see it, and you'd have a faint shadow out in the open. Without one, night is dark except for the lamps above. We've written it with none, from "it should just be darker".
