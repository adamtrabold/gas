# Jobs — gas station

What a person needs to be able to do here. Written from the owner's words; anything I added is marked *inferred*. Say "wrong" or "missing" against any line.

## What the owner's answers changed

- "Let's not worry about the work right now I just want to nail the world." So the jobs below are about the place. The work is parked, not dropped: the world keeps room for it.
- "Mainly it's for me … I always wanted to own an old gas station to make into something." So Adam is the first user, and *making it into something over time* is a job in its own right.
- "We need sound effects at least." Hearing the place is a job now, not a maybe.
- "It definitely needs an arrow sign." The arrow sign is the thing that brings you in.
- "It should feel liminal not funny." Applies to every job.

## Flows

**First arrival.** Open the site → outside, on the road, the station ahead, the arrow sign → something responds when I look or touch, in ink and sound → I know there's a way in.

**Going in and out.** At the door → one deliberate act, about a second → inside → look around, touch things → back out the way I came, or out the back and round to the front.

**Doing nothing.** Stop touching → the light moves, the place makes its own sounds → sooner or later something happens that wouldn't have if I'd kept poking.

**Another hour.** Come back at night → dark, the sign lit, the door still opens → different light, different sounds, different odd things. Inside, the wall clock can be turned by hand to see other hours; it says it's been turned, and lets go when I leave.

**Something alien.** Notice a light, a mark, a shape that was there a moment ago → never the creature itself → it's noted somewhere inside, so I know there are more.

**Sending it.** Copy the address → the other person lands outside, at their own hour.

**When things are missing.** No 3D → a still picture of the station at this hour, and the place described in words. Motion turned off → cuts in place of moves. Sound blocked or off → visibly off, and nothing depends on hearing.

**Adam adds a thing.** Make one object or one event → give it a place and, if wanted, an hour → it's there → nothing else was touched.

**Adam swaps a drawing.** Replace one generated stroke set or one stand-in model with his own → that thing changes everywhere it appears → the rest stays generated until he gets to it.

## Rules these jobs lean on

- Nothing I need is ever locked behind a time, a wait or a secret. Only extras are.
- Every act takes about a second and answers in ink and sound. No puzzles.
- The hour is my real hour. Only the wall clock moves it.
- Sound starts with my first touch, never before, and can be turned off in one place.

## Still open, and still matter

1. **What goes on the arrow sign?** No name yet is fine — but the sign needs a word or it needs to be blank on purpose. Your name, a station name, or nothing for now?
2. **Is it public while it's only the world?** If you'll send people to it before the work is in, should it say whose it is and how to reach you, somewhere small? Or is it just yours for now?
3. **Walk around freely, or move between a few composed views?** "Threejs" could mean either, and it changes jobs 4, 5 and 12 more than anything else.
4. **Does the place have its own sound when I'm doing nothing** — wind, a hum, a radio somewhere — or only sounds I cause?
