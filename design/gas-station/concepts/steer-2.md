# Steer 2 — design director

After the designers' second pair of turns. No new owner answers; the canopy question is still out.

## How I checked

- Read `00-shared.md`, 01, 02, 03 (both halves), `jobs.md`, and every sheet including `swim-test.png`.
- **Walked the prototype myself** this time: `sketches/place/` in a headless browser at phone size (390 × 844), live, not stills. Three walks: concept 1 mid-morning, concept 3 at night, concept 3 mid-afternoon. Each: arrive, hold forward for 3.5 seconds, drag to turn, walk again, stand still for 9 seconds, with a frame captured at each step.
- What that is and isn't: it is a real walk through the real-time scene, and I looked at consecutive frames. It is not a phone in a hand. It ran at about 9 to 10 frames a second in software rendering, which says nothing reliable about a real phone either way.

## What I found walking it

- **The ink stays put.** Shadow shapes and the lamp pool hold their shape from frame to frame as the view moves. I agree with the swim test's conclusion for what it measures. The largest risk from last round is down from "every concept fails" to "unproven on a device".
- **There are no brush strokes in the walked world.** Edges are noise fibres; masses are flat fills. It is a thresholded 3D scene with ragged edges. Last round's 2D stills had strokes and couldn't be walked; this round's can be walked and have none. Nobody has both, the visual designer says so, and it is now the largest open thing.
- **On a 390-wide phone the live arrival cuts the name off.** I saw "ABOLD" at the left edge. The stills (300 × 650) frame it; the live prototype at a common phone size doesn't. Job 2 is reading his name on arrival.
- **Three seconds into walking toward the door, the sign has left the top of the screen** and the picture is a thin strip of building in a large blank field. That is fine as Ruscha. It means the building has to hold the picture alone once you're moving, and in boxes it only just does.
- **Under the canopy by day is one black mass.** The pumps are gone. The "paper line for near things in shade" rule isn't built and the place needs it.

## Rulings

**02 folds.** The kill condition was mine and the frames fail it: two of the four awake things drawn can't be told, one is always black anyway, and the shadow is a patch for nine months of the year. The systems designer's "01's place plus one rule" is an honest description, and a rule isn't a concept. This was my idea's last descendant. It is done.

Into `00-shared.md`, as things that are true of the place in every concept:
- The sign's shadow is an arrow on the slab, because the sun makes it one.
- On winter middays it comes in through the door (`02-door-out`). A thing to find. It is one of the best frames of the round and it costs nothing.
- The wall clock swings it.
- One cue kept from "awake": **at midday the door is off the latch.** The one hour the place lets you in without asking. An extra, nothing depends on it.
"Awake", the five bearings and the second band are dropped. `02-the-arrow.md` gets a first line saying it is folded and where its parts went.

**Canopy west of the door.** You're both right and my steer was wrong. A canopy in front of the door would catch the sign's shadow on its roof and keep the door in shade all day. `place-day-road` reads as a gas station. Still the owner's building; his answer may remove it.

**The third margin item** (opens the list of places). Accepted. Job 12 needs it.

**The east window and the bench.** Window accepted; the room needs a view of the lot. The bench can stay as furniture. Nothing depends on it.

**01's look back.** Do what the visual designer says: the counter's tube lights the counter and little else, so the front window is the bright thing from the far end of the aisle. And a light at the mouth of the aisle that can be seen from the door.

**01's first rung.** Forty seconds is too long before the place does anything. First small thing at 15 seconds.

**Twilight.** The sky inked in from the top with the letters coming on one at a time belongs to the place, not to 03. Move it to the shared file.

**Landed words.** DING is his own reference (HONK) and stays. CLICK and CLUNK on every switch is a comic strip, and he said "liminal not funny". Rule: a landed word is rare. The bell has one. Switches have their light and their sound and no word.

## Are there still two concepts?

Honestly: barely.

- 03 already walks 01's aisle and stands in the shared place. Its day is, in the visual designer's words, the shared place plus a shadow.
- What only 01 has is the place acting when you stop (the timed list), Call answered by nobody, and the chart.
- Nothing in 03 excludes any of that. Nothing in 01 gives the visitor a hand beyond a tap.

Last round I told you not to blend, so that each could be seen standing whole. That was right for one round and the result is clear: each stands on half a day. I'm ending the building and scoring both as they are, because the scores say this more usefully than another steer would.
