# Elements — what each thing needs before it's drawn

Your words: "before drawing elements let's first establish a list of things they need."

**How I've read that (not confirmed — say if it's wrong):** a list of the elements the place is made of, and for each one the parts it has, agreed with you before anyone draws it. The clock on the pump is the lesson. Nothing gets invented at drawing time.

**How to use it:** against any line say *wrong*, *missing* or *strike*.

**Every part has a tag saying where it came from:**

- **[02]** etc. — one of your pictures, by number
- **[you]** — your words
- **[concept]** — Left on needs it to do something
- **[saved]** — the sign article you saved (we only have a summary; unchecked)
- **PROPOSED** — we made it up. Yours to accept or strike

Each element ends with an empty slot for the visual designer (material, paper grain, kinds of mark). I've left those empty on purpose.

## The order to build in, and why

1. **Pump** — already under your eye, and it's the smallest thing that has steel, glass, rubber and lettering on it. It settles how a made object is drawn.
2. **Slab and island** — everything stands on it and every shadow lands on it.
3. **Canopy** — makes the shade the pumps stand in. The hard case: a thing in shade in front of shade.
4. **Station front** — wall, door, windows, garage door.
5. **Sign** — your name. The biggest thing, and the one with the most unknowns.
6. **Road**
7. **Desert, horizon, sky**
   *— first whole picture: arriving by day —*
8. **Your shadow**
9. **The station's own lights**
10. **Your lamp**
    *— second whole picture: arriving at night —*
11. **Small things on the lot**
12. **Marks on the ground**
13. **The door, and the front room**
14. **Things on the counter and walls**
15. **The aisle**
16. **Round the back**

Outside before inside, because your pictures are all outside and you start outside. Day before night, because night is made of the same things with different light.
