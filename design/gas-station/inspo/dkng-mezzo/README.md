# The owner's grain reference: DKNG Studios, "Mezzo"

Source: https://www.dkngstudios.com/store/p/mezzo-editable-grain-textures (and https://www.dkngstudios.com/store). The owner: "Look at how like dkng studios does their grain… this is how ill be redrawing it" (`owner.md` section 46).

`m1.jpg`–`m3.jpg` are the product's own preview images, reduced. They belong to DKNG Studios; see `../COPYRIGHT-NOTICE.md`. His phone screenshots of DKNG's work are `../26.jpg`–`30.jpg`.

What the page says the product is: "editable mezzotint textures for Adobe Illustrator" with "fine and coarse grain sizes, 10 graphic styles, and smooth fills"; "editable textures using gradients, fills and strokes"; "two texture sizes (Fine and Coarse)".

What the previews show (orchestrator's notes for the team):
- Flat, hard-edged shapes. Tone inside a shape is a gradient made of grain: irregular specks, dense to sparse, no regular screen.
- Cast shadows are flat darker shapes; the grain softens one edge of a tone, not the shape's outline.
- Two grain sizes: a fine one for smooth gradients, a coarser one where texture is wanted. Both are far smaller than our dots at phone size.
- No outlines.
- m1 happens to be a roadside sign: letters one to a box, on poles, with a starburst.
